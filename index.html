Overview

cros-wa11pap3r displays a fullscreen image in Chrome and keeps it behind a new window — creating the appearance of a custom wallpaper without changing system settings. Works quickly and reliably on Chromebooks.

Automatic (Cloud) — Recommended

  1. Open the hosted helper (opens in a new tab).
  2. Upload a .jpg, .jpeg, or .png.
  3. Helper fits the image, requests fullscreen, and shows a final popup to open a new Chrome window from the taskbar.

This flow is the easiest for casual users and is tuned for performance.

Automatic (Local)

  1. Download the helper HTML from Releases and open it in Chrome.
  2. Upload an image and follow the on-screen steps.

Use this when you want an offline copy or to self-host the helper.

Manual Method

  1. Open an online image in a new tab and zoom until it fills the window.
  2. Press Fullscreen (F11) and then open a new Chrome window from the taskbar.

Manual works if you prefer not to use the helper tool.
