cros-wa11pap3r

A friendly, modern way to personalize managed Chromebooks — safely and simply.

Overview

cros-wa11pap3r lets you display your favorite image as a background on managed Chrome OS devices — without developer mode or system modifications. It’s entirely browser-based, reversible, and secure.

Automatic Method (Recommended)

Use the helper tool to easily set your wallpaper through your browser.

Open cros-wa11pap3r Helper
  1. Upload a .jpg, .jpeg, or .png image.
  2. The helper displays it fullscreen and guides you with an on-screen popup.
  3. Right-click Chrome in your taskbar and select New window.

Manual Method

  1. Find a web-accessible image you like and open it in a new tab.
  2. Zoom in until it fills your Chrome window.
  3. Press the Fullscreen key.
  4. Move your mouse to the bottom, right-click Chrome, and choose New window.

Want to try it offline?

Download the local version from GitHub Releases and open it in Chrome.
