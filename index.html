cros-wa11pap3r

Give your managed Chromebook a personal touch — securely, beautifully, and effortlessly.

Launch Helper Tool

Overview

cros-wa11pap3r lets you show your favorite image as a wallpaper on managed Chrome OS devices — all inside Chrome, no system changes or dev mode required. It’s fast, private, and reliable even on low-end hardware.

Automatic Setup (Recommended)

  1. Open the cloud helper.
  2. Upload a .jpg, .jpeg, or .png.
  3. The helper goes fullscreen, fits the image, and guides you with a small popup.

Manual Setup

  1. Find a hosted image you like and open it in a new tab.
  2. Zoom in until it fills your Chrome window.
  3. Press Fullscreen.
  4. Right-click Chrome on your taskbar → New window.

Offline Option

Prefer to keep it local? Download the standalone version and run it directly in Chrome.
