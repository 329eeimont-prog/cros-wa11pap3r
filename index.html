cros-wa11pap3r

Silky-smooth control for personalizing managed Chromebooks.

Overview

cros-wa11pap3r gives you a way to express yourself — even on managed Chrome OS devices. No developer mode, no system hacks. Just a bit of silky, browser-based flow that sets your image fullscreen and lets you keep it behind a new window.

Automatic (Cloud) — Recommended

Start instantly with the hosted helper below:

Launch cros-wa11pap3r Helper
  1. Upload your .jpg, .jpeg, or .png file.
  2. The helper fills your screen with it, in full, silky clarity.
  3. Follow the smooth pop-up guide to finish your setup.

Manual Method

  1. Open your chosen image in a new browser tab (must be online).
  2. Zoom or pinch until it fills the entire window.
  3. Press Fullscreen (or F11).
  4. Right-click the Chrome icon on your taskbar → New window.
